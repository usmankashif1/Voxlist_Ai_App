# Voxlist Ai Support

Use this document to handle customer support and issue reporting for the App.

## Contact

- Email: support@voxlistai.app
- App support link: https://your-domain.example/support
- Privacy policy: ../docs/privacy-policy.md

## Support topics

Please contact us for help with:

- account login or sign-up issues
- task sync problems
- reminder or notification failures
- voice input or AI task extraction issues
- request to delete account data
- report a harmful or inaccurate AI-generated result

## Response times

We aim to respond within 2–5 business days. Response times may vary during periods of high demand.

## Required user-facing disclosures

For Google Play and app review readiness, keep the support contact and privacy policy easily accessible from the App, the Play Store listing, and any web-based resources.
