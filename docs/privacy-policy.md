# Voxlist Ai Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

This Privacy Policy explains how Voxlist Ai (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

## Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- Account information: email address, sign-in status, and account identifiers used by Supabase authentication.
- Task data: title, notes, due dates, reminders, completion status, and related task metadata that you create in the App.
- Voice input data: audio recordings, transcription text, and AI-extracted task drafts when you use voice features.
- Device and app metadata: app version, device model, operating system, notification settings, and basic diagnostics needed to keep the app working reliably.
- Usage data: feature usage, timestamps, and crash/error diagnostics to improve stability.

## How we use the information

We use the information to:

- create and manage your account
- save and sync your tasks across devices
- process voice recordings for transcription and task extraction
- send reminders and notifications you enable
- diagnose bugs and improve app performance
- comply with legal and security obligations

## Third-party services

The App may use the following services:

- Supabase for authentication, database storage, and account/session management.
- Google Gemini and Groq for voice transcription and AI task extraction.
- Expo/React Native platform services for notifications and secure local storage.

These services may process data on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## Voice and AI processing

If you use voice features, audio is sent to our transcription and AI services so we can convert the recording into text and extract tasks. We do not use voice input for unrelated profiling or advertising.

We keep only the data needed to provide the service. If a voice file is temporary, it should be removed promptly after processing. If you have questions about retention or deletion, contact us using the support information below.

## Data retention

We retain:

- account data as long as your account remains active
- task data as long as you keep the task or until you delete your account
- voice and AI-related data only for the time needed to provide the functionality and troubleshoot issues

## Your rights and choices

You may:

- review and update your account details in the App
- delete your tasks or account
- stop using voice features or notifications
- request account deletion and related data removal

If you want to delete your account or request data removal, use the in-app delete account flow or contact support at support@voxlistai.app.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No system is completely risk-free, so please keep your login credentials secure.

## Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, or under the age required by local law, without parental consent.

support@voxlistai.app

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
