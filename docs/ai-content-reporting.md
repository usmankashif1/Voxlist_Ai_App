# AI Content Reporting

This App may generate AI-assisted task suggestions and transcription output. Users must be able to report harmful, offensive, or inaccurate content.

## Reporting flow

Use the in-app report flow or contact support to report:

- offensive or unsafe AI output
- inaccurate task suggestions
- hallucinated or misleading content
- abuse or content that violates product policies

## Reporting contact

- Email: support@voxlistai.app
- App data: report from the Settings screen when available

## Needed information

Please include:

- the related task title or prompt context
- a short summary of the issue
- the AI output that was problematic
- how serious the issue is

## Review

Reports are reviewed to improve safety, prompt quality, and user trust. We may take action such as removing the offending content, disabling a result, or updating the prompt logic.
